## Add a schedule

Open **Scheduled** and choose **Add**. Enter the payment details, amount, account, due date, and recurrence. Review the start and end dates where applicable before saving.

A schedule describes planned cashflow. Recording or linking the actual transaction is a separate step.

## Choose the view you need

- **Upcoming** helps you review unpaid expenses due in the next seven calendar days, including today.
- **Monthly** groups the selected month’s occurrences by date and shows payment progress.
- **All** lets you find and edit your schedules, including inactive ones.

## Mark an occurrence paid

For an unpaid occurrence, choose **Mark paid**. For income, use **Mark received**. Depending on the selected flow, you can:

1. **Create a transaction** when the payment has not been recorded yet.
2. **Link an existing transaction** if you already entered it.
3. **Mark paid only** to update the occurrence without creating a transaction.

For flexible schedules, enter the actual amount when creating the transaction.

> If you already recorded a bill manually, link that transaction. Creating another record would count the same payment twice.

## Check or correct a payment

Use **View expense** on a linked occurrence to inspect its transaction. The payment’s additional options include marking it unpaid again when appropriate; review the confirmation carefully.

## Add a monthly plan

If **Budgets** is enabled in your app, use it to allocate amounts to monthly categories and compare the plan with completed expenses. Transfers and income are separate from category spending. Some features may depend on your installed version and configuration.
