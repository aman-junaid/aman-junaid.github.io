## Accounts answer “where?”

Use an account for the place money comes from or goes to. Your bank account and your cash wallet are useful examples. Review **Accounts** to see account-specific activity.

When moving money between your own accounts, use a **Transfer** with a source and destination account. This keeps a cash withdrawal separate from spending that cash later.

## Categories answer “what kind?”

Choose a category that describes the purpose of an expense or income. Consistent categories make the breakdowns in **Insights** easier to interpret.

Start broad. A few categories you use reliably are more useful than a long list you keep changing.

## Labels connect related entries

Labels, also called tags, let you connect transactions across categories. A trip might include food, transport, and accommodation; give those entries a shared trip label to find them together later.

Add or choose labels in the transaction editor. A transaction can have more than one label.

## Find the entries you need

Open the full transaction list from **Home → View all**. Use search and filters to narrow by fields such as account, category, label, date, or amount. Open a matching transaction to review or edit it.

> Try one shared label for your next project or trip. Keep ordinary categories for the type of spending, and use the label to connect the bigger activity.
